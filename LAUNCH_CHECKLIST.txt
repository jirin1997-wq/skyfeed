================================================================================
                         📞 QUICK TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

❌ Frontend shows "API Error"
✅ Check Vercel env var NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL is set correctly

❌ No articles appearing
✅ Backend might be starting - wait 1-2 minutes, then refresh

❌ Maps not loading
✅ Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)

❌ Website links don't work
✅ Make sure you're clicking on the marker popup or "Visit Website" button
